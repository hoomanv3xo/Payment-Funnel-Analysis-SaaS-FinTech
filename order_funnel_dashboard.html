Recommended next actions

Focus first on the observed bottleneck and the risk of incorrectly treating paid customers as unpaid.

01 · INSTRUMENT

Capture the real failure mode

Add payment attempt ID, error source, error code, vendor transaction ID, portal load events, and form-validation events.

02 · RECONCILE

Close the success gap

Auto-retry or queue a reconciliation when a payment succeeds but the internal subscription has not completed within 15 minutes.

03 · RECOVER

Use stage-specific outreach

Send a direct payment link for pre-submit abandonment; use correction guidance for validation issues and clear status messaging for processor errors.
